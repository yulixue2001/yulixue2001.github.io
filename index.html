About

I am a PhD student concentrating in Environmental Economics in the Department of Agricultural and Applied Economics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My fields of interest include environmental and public economics, urban economics, and empirical industrial organization.

Before Madison, I received an MSc in Environmental Economics and Climate Change from the London School of Economics and a B.S. in Environmental Economics and Policy from the University of California, Berkeley.
